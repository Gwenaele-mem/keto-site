Silky chocolate-avocado shake

25 min · 1 servings · Medium

125kcal
2.3 gnet carbs
3.5 gprotein
10.6 gfat

Per serving (294 g) · 0.8 g net carbs per 100 g

Gluten-freeLactose-freeHalalKosherContains: almonds

Ingredients

for 1 servings

  • Avocado50 g (1.8 oz)
  • Unsweetened almond milk180 g (6.3 oz)
  • Unsweetened cocoa8 g
  • Erythritol6 g
  • Iced water50 g (1.8 oz)

Directions

  1. Halve the avocado, remove the stone and scoop out the flesh with a spoon.
  2. Put the avocado flesh in the blender with the unsweetened almond milk, the unsweetened cocoa and the erythritol.
  3. Add the iced water along with a few ice cubes for a really cold drink.
  4. Blend at full speed for 30 to 60 seconds, until thick, smooth and creamy.
  5. Taste, adjust the sweetness if needed, pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.
