Privacy policy

1. Data controller

Personal data processed by the My Keto Assistant application is processed under the responsibility of Gwenaële DOMECQ, sole trader operating under the business name "NovAction Accompagnement" (see Legal notice). Contact for any question or request concerning your data: contact@keto-assistant.eu.

2. Security and encryption

All communications between the App and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Your password is hashed with the bcrypt algorithm by our authentication provider: nobody — not even us — can read it. Each account can access only its own data (security rules applied row by row in the database).

3. Data we collect

• Account: e-mail address and password (hashed). • Profile: your sign-up answers — sex, age, height, weight, goal, activity level, chosen diet, food preferences and exclusions, declared contraindications. • Diary: food eaten, water, weight, body measurements, exercise, sleep, fasting, and blood ketones if you record them. • Menus, recipes and foods you create; coach tracking (weekly points). Some of this data is health data: we process it only to deliver the service (calculating your plan, tracking, personalising your menus) — never for any other purpose.

4. Legal bases

Processing is based on: performance of the contract (providing the service you subscribed to), your consent for the health data you enter and for the optional connections (Apple Health / Health Connect), and our legitimate interest in the security of the service. You may withdraw a consent at any time.

5. Where is your data stored?

On Supabase, hosted within the European Union (AWS Paris region, eu-west-3). Some convenience settings (favourites, entry history, shopping list ticks, display preferences) stay stored locally on your device.

6. Payments

Subscriptions are collected by Apple's App Store or Google Play: no banking data passes through our servers or is stored there. The platforms pass on to us only the subscription status (active, expired, cancelled) needed to unlock your features.

7. Connected health (optional)

On mobile, you can connect the App to your phone's health record (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android) to import your steps, active calories and sleep automatically. This connection is optional, READ-only, enabled only with your explicit agreement through the system dialog, and revocable at any time (in the App or in your phone's settings). The App writes nothing to your health record.

8. What we do NOT do

• No selling or sharing of your data with third parties. • No advertising, no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics tools. • No external artificial intelligence: none of your data is sent to an AI service. • No use of your health data for any purpose other than the service. Recipes you choose to share with the community are visible to other members, only on your own initiative. The food entries you create (name, brand, barcode, nutritional values) enrich the shared database and are visible to other members: these are product data, never your intake or your identity. They do not carry the "verified values" tick, which is reserved for the official CIQUAL table. Entries created before 28 August 2026 remain private. You alone can edit or delete yours.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting the account (Profile → Account → Delete my account) erases your personal data and your login account. Your community contributions (shared recipes, forum posts) may be kept in anonymised form. Technical backups are purged according to our host's cycle.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. The App provides directly: a full export of your data (Profile → Account → Export my data, JSON format) and account deletion. For any other request: contact@keto-assistant.eu — answered within 30 days at the latest. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

11. Minors

The App is reserved for adults (18 and over); sign-up refuses any lower age. If we discover that a minor's data has nonetheless been collected, we delete it without delay. Parents: contact@keto-assistant.eu.

12. Health warning

My Keto Assistant is a nutrition tracking tool, not a medical device. The plans it offers are no substitute for medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before starting a ketogenic diet, in particular in the event of illness or medical treatment.

13. Updates to this policy

The version in force is the one shown in the App (date at the top of the page). In the event of a substantial change — for example the addition of a new connected service — you will be informed in the App.
